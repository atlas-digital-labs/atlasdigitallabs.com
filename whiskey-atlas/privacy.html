Whiskey Atlas Privacy Policy

Atlas Digital Labs LLC | Updated October 22, 2026

Information we keep

Whiskey Atlas creates an account identifier and username when you first use the app. We save your profile photos, collection and wish list, quantities and prices you enter, tasting notes and scores, friendships, room activity and messages, catalog contributions, and support messages. You may provide a recovery email to help regain access to your account.

We record app visits and actions, platform, session identifiers, last-opened time, and certain service errors to understand usage and troubleshoot problems. These records are linked to your account. Notification services use device tokens and app installation identifiers to deliver notifications. Our service providers may process technical connection information, such as IP addresses and device or software information, to operate and secure their services.

How we use it

We use this information to maintain your account, provide collections and tastings, personalize your experience, connect friends, run tasting rooms and contribution rewards, understand feature usage, and respond to support requests. Submitted bottle details and photos are reviewed and may become part of the shared Atlas catalog.

What other members can see

Your username, profile images, contribution rank, collection, whiskey shelves, and published tasting reviews are visible to other members, not only friends. Room participants can see what you share in a room.

Atlas Live is a world view available to members. Published tastings show the bottle, score, date, and username, with links to the member's profile and review. The Friends view is a filter, not a privacy setting. Blocking remains available. Unpublished tastings, private collection details such as prices paid, and recovery information are not shared through these community views. Approved catalog details and images are available to the community.

Storage and service providers

We use Supabase for accounts, app data, and uploaded images, and Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple notification services for push notifications. Catalog submissions are processed with automated tools and reviewer access. Service providers process information needed to perform these functions.

We use authenticated access controls and database permissions to limit access to account data. Whiskey Atlas does not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising tracking. External websites and services you open or share to have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, manage your collection and tastings, and export your collection from the Data section of the control center. Camera access is used for scanning and photos you choose to take; uploaded photos are stored when you submit them. Manage camera and notification permissions in your device settings, and alert preferences in the app.

Keeping and deleting data

Saved account data is kept to provide these features until you remove it or delete your account. Use Delete account in the Account section to remove your profile, collection, tastings, and account access. Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Approved contributions to the shared bottle catalog may remain. We retain a record of account deletion requests, including any email and reason supplied, to track completion and resolve failures. Contact us if you need help accessing or deleting your information.

Adults only

Whiskey Atlas is intended for adults of legal drinking age, not children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information.

Contact

For privacy questions or help with your data, email brian@atlasdglabs.com or use Contact Support in the app's global drawer.
